### Architectural Precedent Study
Case Studies (#11), Literature Reviews (#53)

Case studies investigate past projects and literature reviews gather and synthesize existing literature, altogether to inform new investigations. The primary methodology for my design involves the reading and analysis of architectural precedents, including conceptual, competition, and built precedents. Materials for reading include the background and site of the project, the architect's thought process and writing on the project, and plans, sections, and other visual materials. This is especially appropriate for my project as learning about other architects' decisions in negotiating spatial programming, architectural flexibility, and user participation will inform my design choices. Visual materials such as drawings are also important reading material and inspiration as my proposed final deliverables comprise a complete architectural project, requiring a consistent visual language.
### Research Through Design
Research Through Design (#70)

Research Through Design is a principle upon treating the iterative process of design as closely as possible to scientific research. Because my project is mainly an architectural design but also has strong correlations to the sociological, psychological, and behavioral sciences, I believe that it is crucial to approach my project with the perspective of conducting research. Comparisons between iterations will allow the design process itself to refine my hypothesis and assumptions to create a more multidimensional final product.
### Behavioral Mapping
Behavioral Mapping (#6), Participant Observation (#59)

Behavioral mapping records human activity in relation to physical location, and participant observation involves experiencing an environment as an active participant. Both techniques relate physical experience to architecture and are thus relevant to my studies in how people interact with nightlife architecture to create authentic experience. I could conduct a study of a specific existing nightlife venue at location of feasible proximity (Boston, closest by distance or NYC, high density of precedents), documenting how real visitors move and occupy the spaces in relation to architectural details such as circulation and enclosure. Conducting a study of my own will reveal insights on the relationship between intended and actual programming in a way that literature review cannot.
